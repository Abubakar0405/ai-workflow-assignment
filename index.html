Workflow Design & AI Integration

Streamlining Student Admissions via Multimodal OCR & Automated Decision Triage

1. Case Study & As-Is Bottleneck

Process: University Student Admissions & Transcript Verification.

Problem: Admissions officers manually review uploaded transcripts line-by-line, spending 12–15 minutes per file. During peak admissions season, this creates a 3-week waiting backlog and human data-entry errors.

2. The To-Be AI Workflow

  1. Submission: Student uploads transcript PDF/Image.
  2. AI Extraction: Multimodal Vision LLM / OCR reads grades and course titles in under 10 seconds.
  3. Rule Engine: Calculates GPA, verifies prerequisite courses (Math, Physics, English).
  4. Dual Triage: High confidence (≥90%) auto-approves. Low confidence (<90%) routes to staff.
  5. Offer Dispatch: Instant conditional acceptance email sent.

3. Suggested AI Improvements

  • 24/7 RAG Support Bot: Resolves 65% of applicant inquiries about eligibility and deadlines instantly.
  • Predictive Yield Model: Machine learning forecasts admitted student enrollments to optimize class sizes.
